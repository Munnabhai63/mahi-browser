Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

Effective Date: October 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the mandatory rules and ethical standards governing the use of MAHI Browser, its associated cloud profile synchronization, Cloud Browser runtime fleet, REST APIs, and commercial proxy integrations (collectively, the "Services").

1. Authorized Use Cases

MAHI Browser is designed, developed, and licensed exclusively for legitimate, lawful operational activities, including but not limited to:

  • Multi-account digital marketing, e-commerce store administration, and brand asset management.
  • Cross-region QA verification, website localization testing, and ad verification.
  • Public data research, price monitoring, and competitive market intelligence.
  • Privacy-preserving browsing and device fingerprint leak mitigation.

2. Strictly Prohibited Activities

Zero Tolerance Violations: Any account or organization found participating in, facilitating, or encouraging the following activities will face immediate, permanent account termination without refund, API key revocation, and potential referral to relevant law enforcement agencies.

  • Financial Fraud & Carding: Card testing, unauthorized credit card transactions, banking fraud, identity theft, or deceptive payment processing.
  • Credential Stuffing & Brute-Forcing: Automated dictionary attacks, credential harvesting, unauthorized account takeovers (ATO), or access to unowned accounts.
  • Malware & Cyber Attacks: Hosting, transmitting, or distributing spyware, keyloggers, trojans, ransomware, or coordinating botnets or command-and-control infrastructure.
  • Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS): Flooding target network infrastructure, port scanning, exploitation of unpatched vulnerabilities, or disruption of third-party services.
  • Spam & Unsolicited Bulk Messaging: Automated spam transmission across email, messaging apps, forums, or social networks in violation of CAN-SPAM, GDPR, or equivalent regulations.
  • Illicit Proxy Tunneling: Using MAHI Browser proxy mechanisms to tunnel unlawful network traffic, circumvent lawful sanction lists, or route malicious payloads.

3. Cloud Browser & Automation Resource Fair Use

Users granted access to the hosted Cloud Browser fleet and Visual RPA engine must maintain sustainable resource utilization:

  • Cryptocurrency mining, intensive video rendering, or unauthorized distributed compute workloads inside Cloud Browser instances are strictly prohibited.
  • Sessions must not intentionally bypass CPU/RAM limits or spawn unmonitored background fork processes.
  • Automated workflows must respect target servers' robots.txt and reasonable rate-limiting boundaries.

4. Platform Security & Reverse Engineering

You agree not to modify, decompile, disassemble, reverse engineer, or create derivative works of MAHI Browser, nor bypass licensing locks, hardware ID checks, or cryptographic signature verifications.

5. Investigation, Enforcement & Account Suspension

Enforcement Authority: MAHI Browser reserves the unilateral right to investigate any reported or detected violation of this AUP. Upon confirmation of prohibited activity, we may immediately suspend or terminate access, revoke all issued API keys and workspace memberships, and cooperate with law enforcement and affected third parties.

6. Reporting Abuse

If you detect, suspect, or have evidence that MAHI Browser or its infrastructure is being utilized in violation of this Acceptable Use Policy, please report it immediately to our dedicated Trust & Safety team:

Email: abuse@mahibrowser.com
General Support: support@mahibrowser.com

Frequently asked questions related to
antidetect browser

What is MAHI Browser?
MAHI is a Windows anti-detect browser and profile manager. It creates completely isolated Chromium browser environments with unique hardware fingerprints, dedicated proxies, and zero-code automation so you can manage dozens of accounts without bans or account linking.
Does MAHI Browser provide free residential proxies?
MAHI Browser is a universal proxy orchestrator. You can connect any third-party SOCKS5, HTTP, or HTTPS proxies with automated local authentication tunneling and live IP latency diagnostics. We also include a built-in 4G/5G Mobile Hotspot Rotator that cycles airplane mode on your USB-connected Android phone for free mobile carrier IPs.
Is MAHI Browser available on macOS or Linux?
Currently, MAHI Browser is officially available and production-tested for 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11. Native installers for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux are actively under development and will be available in an upcoming release.
How does the Multi-Window Synchronizer work?
The synchronizer connects directly to your launched Chromium profile windows via Chrome DevTools Protocol (CDP). When you navigate, click, or type in your designated Master window, the exact same actions are mirrored in real time across all selected Slave windows simultaneously.
Is my profile data stored safely?
MAHI Browser is local-first and privacy-focused. All your profiles, cookies, and proxy credentials are encrypted with AES-256 and stored on your local PC. You can export encrypted profile packages anytime for portable backup or migration. Centralized multi-device cloud sync is currently in beta.

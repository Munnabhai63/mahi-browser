Normal browsers share your hardware identity (GPU, audio hash, screen metrics) across all open windows and incognito tabs, allowing websites to easily link your accounts. Virtual machines isolate hardware but consume 2–4 GB of RAM each. MAHI Browser runs on a lightweight Chromium engine that spoofs hardware parameters per profile with minimal RAM overhead.
